Research Vision

Systems that learn must also know how far they may change. I aim to build learning-enabled autonomous systems — robots first, and equally vehicles, energy and cyber-physical systems — that feel, believe, adapt and decide under uncertainty, with every change to what they believe certified against the tolerances of what they do. I call this Compliant Intelligence: stability gained by adapting to uncertainty rather than resisting it.

Feel → Believe → Adapt → Decide → Interact

Scientific Research Philosophy

Adaptation bounded by guarantees. My research isolates the belief of a learning-enabled system and asks a fundamental question: how aggressively can this belief adapt, switch, or correct itself without violating the strict safety tolerances of the physical decisions it drives?

  • Dynamic Structure Switching: Certifying the limits of how often a system can change its world model under noisy evidence.

  • Controller-Aware Budgets: Bounding neural observer corrections directly via the robustness margins of Model Predictive Control (MPC).

  • Trust in Latency: Decoding rigorous safety certificates from learned latent states, ensuring resilience even when perception fails.

  • Embodied Validation: Grounding these theoretical guarantees in the physical realities of tactile intelligence and compliant soft robotics.

“Agile in learning. Provable in action.”
