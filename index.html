Research Philosophy

Action is bound by physics; belief must be bound by math. I treat a learning-enabled system's belief as a mathematically constrained structure. My defining question is: how aggressively can an autonomous system adapt, correct, or switch its belief under uncertainty without violating the strict safety tolerances of the physical actions it drives?

“Agile in learning. Provable in action.”

Research Vision

Learning drives adaptation, but guaranteed bounds establish trust:

Compliant Intelligence; Feel → Believe → Adapt → Decide → Interact
