Biography

Dr. Thanana Nuchkrua studies how intelligent systems infer hidden states, adapt their internal models, and make control decisions under uncertainty. His research brings together Bayesian state estimation, stochastic world models, and robust and adaptive control, with applications in robotic manipulation, human-robot interaction, and compliant actuation.

His work follows a connected question: how can an intelligent system revise its belief about the world while keeping that belief useful for control? This perspective links his foundations in soft actuation and nonlinear robot control with his recent research on latent dynamics, structural belief adaptation, and controller-aware inference.

Research Focus

His research on World Models and Control investigates the connections between learned representations, uncertainty, and downstream decisions. Current studies examine how to select latent structures within Bayesian filtering, make adaptation robust to noisy predictive evidence, and propagate uncertainty when observations are incomplete or occluded.

A complementary thread addresses the interface between a learned belief and the physical system: when decoded quantities support a safety certificate, how much a neural observer may correct its belief before an MPC plan changes beyond prescribed tolerances, and how model selection balances switching and recovery. These studies make the assumptions and limits of learning-enabled control explicit.

His longer-term direction is compliant intelligence: connecting tactile perception, stochastic belief, adaptive decision making, and physical interaction. It builds on earlier work in pneumatic artificial muscles, adaptive impedance control, and coordinated robot motion, bringing model-based control theory into dialogue with data-driven learning, reinforcement learning, and Bayesian inference.

Education and International Research Experience

He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering in 2015, with a research focus on soft actuation and control. Since then, his international research appointments have included roles as a Postdoctoral Researcher, Research Associate (Faculty), and Visiting Scholar at:

  • National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan

  • Huazhong University of Science and Technology, China

  • Guangdong Technion-Israel Institute of Technology, China

  • King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi, Thailand

Across these appointments, he has pursued research spanning adaptive robot control, data-driven modeling, soft robotics, and human-robot interaction. His broader interests include shared autonomy and cyber-physical systems.
