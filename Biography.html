Biography

Dr. Thanana Nuchkrua is a researcher in intelligent control systems, with a background in mathematics. His research has evolved from controlling physical motion and interaction to developing Bayesian state estimation and stochastic world models for learning-enabled control. His work spans robotics and cyber-physical systems, with broader interests in understanding and controlling natural and engineered dynamical systems.

Explore the research | Publications

Education and International Research Experience

He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering in 2015 and has held appointments as a Postdoctoral Researcher, Research Associate (Faculty), and Visiting Scholar across National Chung Cheng University (Taiwan), Huazhong University of Science and Technology (China), Guangdong Technion–Israel Institute of Technology (China), and King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (Thailand).

Honors and Fellowships

  • International postdoctoral fellowships in Taiwan, China, and Thailand

  • Visiting Scholar appointments at multiple international research universities

  • Full scholarships for Ph.D. and master's studies

  • Dean's Award: among the top five undergraduate students in Electrical Engineering
